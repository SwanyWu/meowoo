Another unsolicited internet voice

The internet has it all. When I want advice, I go to Reddit. When I want to learn something or get inspired, there’s YouTube. When I want to log movies and read reviews, there’s Letterboxd.

There are communities for almost everything, full of people with more interesting things to say than I do. So why am I starting yet another brain-dump site?

I have scattered thoughts. Some are half-baked observations, some are things I want to learn, and some probably don’t deserve to see the light of day.

The internet doesn’t need another hot take. But sometimes a stranger’s post makes you feel a little less alone, or saves you an afternoon of frustration. Maybe that’s reason enough to share something. I’m not trying to become a thought leader. I just want somewhere to put my thoughts—without algorithms or engagement metrics telling me what deserves attention.

A hobby for unemployment

I’m currently unemployed, which is both a privilege and a strange experience. Having time to explore new things is nice, but too much unstructured time can leave me alone with my own thoughts for a little too long.

I could keep a local journal, but building a brain-dump site gives me something to tinker with too. It pushes me to structure my thoughts, articulate them properly, and maybe make something nice to look at—in case someone stumbles in, even though I’m mostly writing for myself. I can write, code, and occasionally experiment with things. At the very least, it’s something to do besides spiralling in my head.

I want to build something that stays. I’ve built little projects before—browser extensions, Streamlit apps—mostly to solve a problem or showcase my skills. Once the goal was achieved, I moved on.

This time, I want to build something from scratch and maintain it for the foreseeable future. Even when I’m employed again. Yes.

Maybe it’s like growing a plant or keeping a terrarium. You tend to it, watch it change, and enjoy the process without expecting it to become anything extraordinary.

The internet doesn’t need another website. But I think I might enjoy having one.
